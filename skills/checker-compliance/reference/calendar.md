# The calendar that moves sales

Dates for Islamic holidays follow the moon and move about 11 days earlier each
year. The dates below are estimates; the government fixes them with the
three-minister decree on public holidays and joint leave (SKB cuti bersama) and
the sidang isbat. *check* before dating a plan.

## Ramadan and Lebaran

| year | Ramadan starts (about) | Idul Fitri (about) | Idul Adha (about) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2027 | 8 February | 9 to 10 March | 16 to 17 May |
| 2028 | 28 January | 26 to 27 February | 5 May |

What happens to sales:

- **Daytime food and drink** drops during Ramadan; **iftar and sahur** windows
  spike. Takjil (iftar snacks) is its own market.
- **Fashion, gifts, hampers, cookies (kue kering), travel and home goods** peak
  in the last two weeks of Ramadan as THR is paid.
- **Mudik**: in the week before and after Idul Fitri, Jabodetabek and the big
  cities empty out and hometowns fill up. Many staff take leave; some do not come
  back.
- **THR** must be paid at least 7 days before the holiday: a cash spike for the
  business (payroll.md).

When comparing months in `checker/actuals.json`, name the Ramadan, Lebaran and
THR months: a drop there is the calendar, not the business.

## The payday cycle

- Most private-sector salaries arrive between the 25th and the 1st; civil servants
  are paid on the 1st.
- The first week after payday spends most. The lean days before the next payday
  (tanggal tua, roughly the 15th to the 25th) spend least, and promo-hunting peaks.
- Marketplaces run "payday sales" around the 25th.

## Shopping festivals

- **Twin dates**: 1.1, 2.2 ... 9.9, 10.10, **11.11**, **12.12** (Harbolnas, the
  national online shopping day). Big for marketplaces; plan stock and fees.
- Ramadan sales, back-to-school (July), Christmas and New Year, Imlek (Chinese
  New Year: hampers, red packaging, food).

## Seasons and school

- **Rainy season**, roughly November to March or April: foot traffic falls,
  delivery rises, Jakarta and other cities flood. Plan for it in the risk register.
- New school year: July. Universities: August or September. Exams and school
  holidays shift family spending.

## Weekly rhythm

- Offices: Monday to Friday, with work-from-home days in many companies.
- Malls and leisure: Friday evening to Sunday.
- Friday midday: Friday prayers; many men are away from about 11.30 to 13.00.
