# Permits, registrations and rules

Checked November 2026. Items marked *check* change often. Not legal advice: a
notary, the DPMPTSP of the region or the relevant agency confirms.

## Legal form

| form | who it fits | notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual with an NIB | one founder, testing or small | no separate entity; the founder carries the risk |
| PT Perorangan | one founder, micro or small business | a limited company for one person, registered online through AHU (ahu.go.id), no notary deed needed |
| CV | two or more partners | notary deed; active partners carry the risk |
| PT | investors, larger or regulated businesses | notary deed, minimum two shareholders, more reporting |

Tax treatment differs by form (see tax.md).

## OSS-RBA and the NIB

- Every business registers on **OSS** (oss.go.id) and gets an **NIB** (nomor
  induk berusaha). Free. Licensing is risk-based: low-risk activities need only
  the NIB; medium-risk ones add a standard certificate; high-risk ones need a
  licence. Basis: PP 28/2025 on risk-based licensing, which replaced PP 5/2021.
  *check*
- Choose the **KBLI** code (business classification) that matches what is sold.
  The code decides the risk level and the permits. Choose the code OSS shows for
  the activity; a wrong code means wrong permits.
- For an individual, the NIB also registers the business for tax purposes using
  the NIK.

## Food and drink

- **SPP-IRT (PIRT)**: for packaged food made at home or in a small kitchen with a
  shelf life of more than 7 days and a low-risk product type. Applied for through
  OSS, issued by the regency or city, with a food safety training (PKP) step.
- **BPOM MD (local) or ML (imported)**: for packaged food outside the PIRT list
  or above its risk level, and for anything a modern retailer requires.
- **SLHS** (sertifikat laik higiene sanitasi): for restaurants, catering and
  drinking-water depots, from the local health office.
- **Halal** (UU 33/2014, through BPJPH): food and drink sold in Indonesia must be
  halal-certified or say clearly it is not halal. Medium and large businesses
  have been obliged since October 2024. For **micro and small (UMK) food, drink
  and slaughter products, the deadline moved to 17 October 2028** under PP
  40/2026. *check* UMK can use the free **self-declare (Sehati)** route through
  SiHalal (ptsp.halal.go.id) when the product and ingredients qualify.

## Cosmetics, supplements, health products

- Cosmetics need a **BPOM notification**; supplements and traditional medicine
  need BPOM registration. Medical devices go through the Ministry of Health.
- Health claims are tightly limited (see "Advertising" below).

## Premises and signage

- Zoning (KKPR) is checked through OSS for physical premises.
- **Signage (reklame)** needs a regional permit and may carry a regional tax.
- Malls and office buildings often set their own rules (hours, signage, fit-out
  approvals): read the lease.

## Brand

- **Trademark**: search and file with DJKI. Search the register at PDKI
  (pdki-indonesia.dgip.go.id) for the name and close spellings in the same Nice
  classes (for example 30 coffee and tea products, 43 café and restaurant
  services, 35 retail, 3 cosmetics, 25 clothing). UMK pay a reduced fee. *check*
  Indonesia is first-to-file: file before launching publicly.
- **Domains**: .id names through registrars accredited by PANDI (pandi.id). .co.id
  needs company documents; .my.id and .biz.id are cheaper options for
  individuals. Check the .com too.
- **Handles**: Instagram, TikTok, the WhatsApp Business name, Shopee and
  Tokopedia/TikTok Shop store names, the Google Business Profile.

## Selling online

- Selling on marketplaces and social commerce falls under the e-commerce rules
  (PP 80/2019 and Permendag 31/2023 on trading through electronic systems). Sellers
  on a platform follow the platform's rules; running your own large platform needs
  more.
- **Personal data (UU 27/2022, PDP)**: customer phone lists, WhatsApp broadcast
  lists and order data are personal data. Collect with consent, keep them safe,
  let people opt out.

## Advertising and claims

- Consumer protection (UU 8/1999): no misleading claims, prices or "discounts"
  from a price never charged.
- Food, cosmetics and supplements: claims must match what BPOM approved. No
  "cures", "slims", "whitens" unless approved.
- The Indonesian advertising code (Etika Pariwara Indonesia) and platform rules:
  disclose paid endorsements and affiliate links.
- Halal logos only after certification.

## Employing people

- Register employees with BPJS Kesehatan and BPJS Ketenagakerjaan (see payroll.md).
- Report the workforce (WLKP) through the Ministry of Manpower's system.
